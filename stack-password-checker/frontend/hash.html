Hashing is one-way. A hash cannot be decrypted to recover the original message. Use encryption if you need to recover the original data.

Hashing runs in your browser with the Web Crypto API. The text you enter is not sent to the server, stored, or included in the downloaded file.

Good to know

  • The same input always gives the same hash. Changing even one character gives a completely different one.
  • SHA-256 always produces 64 hexadecimal characters (256 bits), SHA-384 gives 96 and SHA-512 gives 128.
  • Do not store passwords as a plain SHA hash. Password storage needs a slow, salted algorithm such as Argon2, scrypt or bcrypt.
