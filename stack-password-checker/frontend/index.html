    Why Password Strength Matters

    • Long passwords are harder to guessEvery extra character multiplies the work an attacker has to do.
    • Unique passwords reduce riskIf one site is breached, your other accounts stay safe.
    • Mix character typesCapitals, numbers and symbols widen the pool of possible passwords.
    • Never reuse important passwordsEmail and banking passwords should never appear anywhere else.
    • Never share passwordsNo legitimate service will ask you for yours.

    Nothing leaves your device

    • Passwords are analyzed in your browser. Only a summary (strength, score, length) is saved to history.
    • Messages and files are encrypted, decrypted and hashed locally with the Web Crypto API.
    • Encryption keys are derived from your password each time. They are never stored or sent.

    Privacy First

    Stack Password Checker analyzes your password for strength. The application should not store or transmit the actual password.
